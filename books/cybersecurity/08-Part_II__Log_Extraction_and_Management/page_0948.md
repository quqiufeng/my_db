Receiving Threat Indicators with Logstash  
 We’ll create an instance of Logstash to receive indicators, either from Filebeat or from custom analyst uploads. It will then format the indicators, add them to the Redis leader cache, and then optionally store them in Elasticsearch. Logstash will act as a  setter  in this role, adding key-value pairs to the Redis leader cache. Later, Logstash  getters  will read these values to compare against streaming data.
