Populating and Replicating Redis for Threat Lookups  
 In this section, you’ll use Filebeat and Logstash to populate a  leader  Redis instance, which will act as the source of key-value pairs containing threat indicators.  Follower  Redis instances will copy, or  replicate , the leader database onto each Logstash node. This design, diagrammed in  Figure 13-1 , lets Logstash check incoming data against an up-to-date cache of malicious indicators, populate alerts, and inform your security operations center. By putting the responsibility of indicator replication solely on Redis, you can ensure that caches remain synchronized after an unexpected outage or reboot.
