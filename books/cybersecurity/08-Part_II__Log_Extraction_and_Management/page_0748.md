Logstash  
 Logstash is a great out-of-the-box Kafka client due to its easy setup and ability to use multiple inputs and outputs simultaneously. Logstash best operates as the “first mile” between programs that can’t connect to Kafka, as the “last mile” between Kafka and Elasticsearch, or between Kafka topics as an enrichment worker that reads one topic and writes to another.
