Viewing Events in Kibana  
 You can view individual event log messages from your hosts and network devices within the Analytics context using Kibana’s Discover feature. Discover lets you see individual events, called  documents  in Elastic parlance, and export them as CSVs or PDFs. Click the hamburger menu, then click    Discover  under the Analytics drop-down menu.
