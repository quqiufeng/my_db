Temporary Data Centralization  
 You may need to centralize your data into one service so that multiple interested parties can receive a copy of it. For example, an organization’s security data might be relevant not only to analysts but also to compliance teams, seasonal auditors, or insider threat teams. Kafka is a platform that links the parties that send the data to the consumers that may need a copy of it and allows analytics processing on the data streaming through it.
