Summary  
 Logstash is a versatile tool that supports receiving a variety of data sources, transforming them as needed, and sending them to many kinds of outputs. In this chapter, you configured several inputs and outputs to explore using Logstash to receive and send data. You also managed multiple pipelines at once to simulate data flowing between disconnected systems and sending data between pipelines on the same system using virtual addressing. 
 This chapter focused on Logstash inputs, outputs, and pipelines. In the next chapter, we’ll examine Logstash filters in depth. Filters are Logstash’s transformational workhorse and the primary source of its value in data engineering and standardization.
