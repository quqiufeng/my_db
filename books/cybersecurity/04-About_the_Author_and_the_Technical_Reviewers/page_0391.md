An Elastic Agent Architecture  
 Elastic Agent runs on endpoints and servers and can operate either as stand-alone, unmanaged log-and-metrics collectors or as members of a centrally managed  fleet : a group of agents that can receive policy updates. The Agent package includes all of the Beats software, such as Filebeat and Packetbeat, and utilizes them through a series of  integrations , or data collection policies.
