Sending Outputs  
 Filebeat can define only one output at a time, leaving very little wiggle room for modifications after you’ve established a pipeline. In most cases, Filebeat   sends data to Logstash or directly to Elasticsearch, but sending data to message brokers such as Kafka and Redis is common, too.
