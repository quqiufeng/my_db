Working with Redis  
 To connect to the Redis command line interface, use  redis-cli . By default, the command connects to  localhost  over TCP, but let’s use the faster Unix socket to better emulate local services connecting to Redis: 
  $  redis-cli -s /var/run/redis/redis-server.sock
