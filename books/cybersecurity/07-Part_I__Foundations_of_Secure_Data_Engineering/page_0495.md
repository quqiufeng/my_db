Replacing characters in messages is useful when you need to redact messages or standardize a property, such as making all hostnames lowercase. As a useful aside, you can also do case-insensitive regular expression comparisons using  re_match_i  or drop all trailing line terminators in the  message  field using  %msg:::drop-last-lf% .
