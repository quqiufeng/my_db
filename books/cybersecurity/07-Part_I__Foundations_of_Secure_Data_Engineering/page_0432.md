 Elasticsearch needs dedicated nodes running the  ingest  role in environments larger than a home lab or small test network. However, you can perform custom transformations using Logstash, a tool we’ll cover in depth in   Chapters 8   and   9  . Using Logstash, you can transform data before it reaches Elasticsearch or another database. This is especially useful if Elasticsearch isn’t the only destination for your data; for instance, you may want to back up data in the cloud, send select logs to an insider threat team, and parse other data that Beats and Elastic Agent don’t support.
