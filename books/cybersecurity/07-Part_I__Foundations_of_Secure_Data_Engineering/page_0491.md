Rulesets  
 In addition to the syntax covered so far, a configuration can use  rulesets  to determine how to process events. Rulesets apply  if - else  statements with various comparison operators, such as  contains_i  for case-insensitive string checks and  re_match  for regular expressions. They typically contain actions to direct traffic to various outputs, such as remote systems or the dynamically named files discussed in the previous section. Rulesets may be bound to an input or called from elsewhere in the configuration, including inside other rulesets.
