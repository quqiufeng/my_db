Equality and Inequality  
 The most basic way to compare fields is with an equality ( == ) or inequality ( != ) check. You can also join multiple comparisons with  and  or use  and not  to exclude the results of a comparison. Parentheses can encapsulate your logic for readability and ensure the logic works as intended. 
 You should wrap strings in quotation marks, but not integers or floats. Reference fields using their square bracket notation, without quotation marks, and place each nested field in its own subsequent bracket: 
  [event][code] == 11
[event][code] == 11.0
[event][code] != "eleven"
[http][request][method] == "POST" and [http][response][status_code] == 200
[user][name] == "bob" and not ([event][code] == 11 and [host][name] == "bobpc")
