Local Files  
 The  imfile  module reads from local files. By default, local files have the facility  local0  and the severity  notice , but you can specify new settings on a per-file basis if needed, as we do in the following: 
  module(load="imfile")
input(
    type="imfile"
    file="/var/log/myapp.log"
    tag="myapp:"
    facility="local1"
    severity="info"
    freshStartTail="on"
)
