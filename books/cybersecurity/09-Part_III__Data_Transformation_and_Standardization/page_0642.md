When you’re performing regular expression matches, the case-insensitive mode modifier  (?i)  often comes in handy. Everything to the right of the modifier gets matched as case-insensitive, and everything to the left remains case-sensitive: 
  # Only "WORLD" will be case-insensitive.
[message] =~ /HeLlO| (?i) WORLD/
# Both words will be case-insensitive.
[message] =~ / (?i) HeLlO|WORLD/
