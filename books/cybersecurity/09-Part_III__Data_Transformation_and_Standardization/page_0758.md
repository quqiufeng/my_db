Commands, Playbooks, and Roles  
 Ansible modules perform one or more specific tasks, such as copying files, managing services, or updating access lists. You may execute one task at a   time using ad hoc commands or perform a collection of tasks in sequence using a  playbook . Playbooks execute one or more tasks at a time during a run, or  play . You may also use playbooks to run multiple other playbooks at once. 
 To further abstract these components, you could use  roles , or reusable, grouped bundles of variables, tasks, playbooks, and other items. We won’t use roles in this chapter, but you could use the content you’ll create in this chapter to make your own roles. You can find useful examples in the “Roles” section of the Ansible documentation.
