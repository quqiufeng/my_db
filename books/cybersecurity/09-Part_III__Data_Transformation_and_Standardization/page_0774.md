Running Ad Hoc Commands  
  Ad hoc commands  are single command lines that use one module at a time to perform one specific task. This might include copying a file to several hosts, updating system packages, or restarting services on a specific group of servers. Ad hoc commands are useful for making unexpected or minor changes to multiple servers at a time, performing routine maintenance, and running individual shell commands. In this section, we consider examples of common ad hoc commands.
